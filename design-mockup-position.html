Where you'd stand, and what's open

Two questions that were never really separate. A position is a rank inside a country's own published wage table — not an estimate of you. An estimate is this pipeline's own model, shown only ever beside the distribution it came from. Openings are real advertisements, with the employer's own range where they published one. Where a country cannot answer one of these, it says which one and why, before you spend effort on it.

Three fields, and a CV upload will fill these same three later — nothing on this page assumes one exists. Experience only changes the position where a country publishes an age or experience cross; everywhere else it is recorded and openly ignored, which the country's own row says.

Where this works, before you invest effort

Four axes. The first three are what packages 7–11 established about whether a country can be compared at all; the fourth is whether anyone is actually advertising. A country can answer the position question perfectly and have nothing open, or the reverse. Click a row to open it.
